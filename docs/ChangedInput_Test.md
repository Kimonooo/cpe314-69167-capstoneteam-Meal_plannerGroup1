# The CHANGED INPUT test (Ken + Venice, Day 2)
Why: the grader changes the data and checks that the chart changes. This test proves our chart is real.
Do it on Kimberly's laptop while she shares her screen. You tell her what to click.

## The 5 steps
| # | What to click | What you should see | Write it down |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Open the app (or `Milestone2Preview`). Click the **Analytics** tab. Press **Refresh**. | A chart and three number cards. | Write the START numbers for Breakfast, Dessert, MainCourse and TOTAL. Take a screenshot: **Figure BEFORE**. |
| 2 | Click the **Recipes** tab. Click the **chicken** quick-search button. Click any recipe in the list. | Its details appear on the right. | Write down its category chip (for example Chicken = MainCourse). |
| 3 | Click **Add to my plan** THREE times (change the date with the date picker between clicks if you like). | A green message "Added ..." each time. The "Planned for ..." list shows the meals. | |
| 4 | Click the **Analytics** tab again. | The tab refreshes by itself. | Write the AFTER numbers. Take a screenshot: **Figure AFTER**. |
| 5 | Compare. | The category of that recipe went up by exactly 3, and TOTAL went up by exactly 3. | Pass or Fail? |

If the bars did not move, tell Kimberly right away. That would be a real bug.
